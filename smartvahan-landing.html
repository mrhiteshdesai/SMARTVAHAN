About SmartVahan

India’s Intelligent
Reflective Tape
Compliance Platform

SmartVahan is a QR Code-based MIS that monitors and enforces installation of government-mandated, BIS-certified reflective tapes on commercial vehicles — as required under CMVR 1989, Rule 104-A to 104-D.

The platform generates tamper-proof certificates, auto-syncs with Vahan, and enables real-time enforcement — eliminating counterfeit fitments across India’s roads.

QR-Based Traceability

Every tape roll gets a unique, state-locked, brand-bound QR — impossible to duplicate or reuse.

Digital Certificates + Vahan Sync

Auto-generated PDF certificates with geo-tags and photos, pushed directly to Vahan portal.

Anti-Counterfeit Enforcement

RTO officers verify tape authenticity in seconds via SmartScanner app at any checkpoint.

How It Works

From QR Generation to RTO Verified

A fully digital, tamper-proof compliance chain — from manufacturer to enforcement officer.

01

QR Code Generated

SmartVahan MIS assigns a unique state+brand+serial encoded QR to each tape roll — impossible to duplicate.

02

OEM Affixation

Brand/OEM embeds QR with 3D holographic + UDV resin tamper-evident seal at the point of manufacturing.

03

Authorised Distribution

Licensed distributors receive serialised stock. Every distribution event is logged in MIS for traceability.

04

Fitment & Certificate

Licensed dealer installs tape, scans QR, captures 4 photos, enters vehicle data — PDF cert generated instantly.

05

Vahan Portal Sync

Certificate auto-pushed to NIC Vahan portal — tamper-proof, zero manual entry, visible to all RTOs.

06

RTO Verification

RTO officer scans QR via SmartScanner — instant VERIFIED, PENDING, or INVALID response with full details.
